# Life Cycle Assessment

## Definition
Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) is a systematic, analytical method used to evaluate and quantify the environmental impacts of a product, process, or service throughout its entire life cycle—from raw material extraction and manufacturing to distribution, use, and end-of-life disposal or recycling.

## Key Characteristics
- **Systems Thinking**: Evaluates impacts across the entire value chain rather than focusing on a single stage of production.
- **Quantitative Measurement**: Provides metrics for environmental burdens such as greenhouse gas emissions, energy consumption, and toxicity.
- **Strategic Tool**: Supports data-driven decision-making for product design and environmental regulatory compliance.
- **Iterative Process**: Often used in conjunction with continuous improvement cycles to reduce ecological footprints over time.

## Applications
- **Semiconductor Manufacturing**: Measuring the environmental footprint of chip fabrication, including hazardous waste output and electricity usage.
- **ESG Accountability**: Providing verifiable data for Environmental, Social, and Governance reporting and global climate goal alignment.
- **Sustainable Design**: Facilitating the implementation of [[concepts/safe-and-sustainable-by-design|Safe and Sustainable by Design]] (SSbD) principles.
- **Innovation Management**: Integrating digital circularity and zero-waste engineering practices into product development.
