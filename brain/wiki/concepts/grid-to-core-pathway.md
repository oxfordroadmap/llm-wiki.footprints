# Grid-to-Core Pathway

## Definition
The grid-to-core pathway is a proposed integration strategy designed to link industrial energy consumption data at the facility level directly to process-level [[concepts/virtual-metrology|Virtual Metrology]] loops. By embedding power electronics and energy footprint metrics into real-time fabrication monitoring, it enables accurate emissions reporting aligned with [[concepts/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam|CBAM]] mandates.

## Key Characteristics
- Direct integration of facility-level power consumption data with process-level monitoring.
- Utilizes power electronics and energy footprint metrics as high-fidelity inputs for manufacturing intelligence.
- Facilitates structural alignment between energy efficiency goals and semiconductor process optimization.
- Enables provenance-aware data fabrics through the integration of [[concepts/virtual-metrology|Virtual Metrology]] and [[concepts/federated-machine-learning|Federated Machine Learning]].

## Applications
- Real-time emissions reporting for regulatory compliance, specifically for [[concepts/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam|CBAM]].
- Enhancing manufacturing intelligence by treating energy transparency as a core operational parameter.
- Closing the efficiency gap between site-wide energy usage and localized semiconductor process steps.
