## Description
The Data Sovereignty Paradox describes the structural tension between the external demand for transparency—often driven by ESG audits and regulatory compliance—and the internal imperative to maintain secrecy over core competitive advantages. Organizations participating in complex digital supply chains often find that the requirements for data interoperability conflict with the necessity of keeping sensitive process logic local and private. This paradox acts as a primary bottleneck for agentic, automated manufacturing environments where high-fidelity data exchange is needed for quality assurance. To resolve this, architectural patterns leverage hardware-isolated enclaves and cryptographic verification to export only necessary compliance proofs rather than raw telemetry, decoupling data utility from data exposure.
