# esg-compliance

### Definition
ESG compliance refers to the process by which an organization ensures its operations, policies, and investments align with established Environmental, Social, and Governance criteria. It involves adhering to regulatory requirements and voluntary frameworks designed to promote corporate sustainability, ethical labor practices, and transparent management structures.

### Key Characteristics
*   **Environmental Impact**: Monitoring carbon footprints, waste management, and energy efficiency.
*   **Social Responsibility**: Ensuring fair labor practices, diversity, and community engagement.
*   **Governance Standards**: Implementing transparent reporting, board diversity, and anti-corruption policies.
*   **Standardization**: Relying on data-driven metrics to report performance to stakeholders and regulatory bodies.

### Applications
Organizations use ESG compliance to attract institutional investors who prioritize sustainable long-term value. It is also applied in supply chain management to ensure that partners, such as those involved in manufacturing like [[entities/tsmc|TSMC]], meet sustainability benchmarks. Furthermore, it serves as a risk management tool to mitigate legal penalties and reputational damage associated with non-compliance in global markets.
