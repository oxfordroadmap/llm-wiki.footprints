# Data Sovereignty

## Definition
Data Sovereignty is the principle that organizations and individuals retain control over how their data is accessed, processed, and shared within and across networks. It ensures that data subjects or owners maintain the authority to enforce usage policies regardless of where the data resides or is processed.

## Key Characteristics
- **Control**: Ability to define, enforce, and revoke usage policies for data assets.
- **Interoperability**: Facilitates secure data exchange between disparate corporate and industrial ecosystems.
- **Trust**: Enables collaboration among partners without requiring total transparency of proprietary or sensitive underlying data.
- **Compliance**: Supports regulatory adherence by ensuring data processing occurs according to established governance rules.
- **Auditability**: Provides mechanisms to verify that data usage aligns with predefined terms and conditions.

## Applications
- **Cross-border Data Sharing**: Enabling international industrial collaboration while respecting regional data protection requirements.
- **Sensitive Telemetry**: Allowing the analysis of industrial data (such as environmental impact metrics) without exposing proprietary industrial secrets or intellectual property.
- **Industrial Ecosystems**: Powering secure communication between semiconductor and petrochemical facilities to optimize shared supply chain efficiencies.
- **Regulatory Reporting**: Assisting in ESG reporting and compliance by providing verifiable data access trails.
