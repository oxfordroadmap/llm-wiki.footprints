# Relay Race Framework

## Definition
The Relay Race Framework is a procedural design pattern for coordinated autonomous governance where professional proxy agents pass data batons across defined layers of an infrastructure stack. It replaces centralized, siloed control with automated, verifiable hand-offs to manage transitions from physical edge telemetry to macro-level financial reporting.

## Key Characteristics
- **Automated Hand-offs**: Uses verifiable data transfer between agents rather than centralized human intervention.
- **Synchronization Boundaries**: Defines clear points of transition where data is validated and translated between functional domains.
- **Domain-Specific Governance**: Facilitates data flow between distinct operational layers, such as facility management, engineering, and finance.
- **Risk Mitigation**: Operationalizes a sequence of checks to ensure compliance is systematically maintained throughout the data lifecycle.

## Applications
- **Semiconductor Manufacturing**: Managing the transition from machine-level physical telemetry to sustainable reporting through [[concepts/safe-and-sustainable-by-design|Safe and Sustainable by Design]] processes.
- **Industrial Data Orchestration**: Automating compliance and reporting workflows within complex [[concepts/international-data-spaces|International Data Spaces]] architectures.
- **Cross-Layer Governance**: Aligning engineering metrics with macro-level financial and sustainability disclosures.
