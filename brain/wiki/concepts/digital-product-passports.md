## Description
The Digital Product Passport (DPP) acts as a critical vehicle for data sharing within complex industrial ecosystems, enabling automated compliance and transparent traceability across global value chains. By centralizing disparate data points, it bridges the gap between upstream production complexity and downstream operational requirements. As a key instrument in modern industrial policy, the DPP facilitates the verification of sustainability metrics and promotes accountability in multi-stakeholder environments. It is often integrated into secure data exchange architectures to ensure that sensitive product information remains accessible, verifiable, and interoperable while maintaining data sovereignty.
