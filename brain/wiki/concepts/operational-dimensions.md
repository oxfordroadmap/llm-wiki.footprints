# Operational Dimensions

## Definition
Operational Dimensions are a set of 10 distinct strategic areas identified within the KPMG Global Business Services (GBS) framework, designed to help organizations assess, calibrate, and enhance their GBS maturity levels.

## Key Characteristics
- **Structured Assessment**: Provides a framework for evaluating the current state of GBS functions.
- **Multidimensional Coverage**: Encompasses core business areas including delivery and sourcing strategy, data and analytics, talent management, process excellence, and enterprise service governance.
- **Analytics-Driven**: Each dimension is supported by robust data and analytics to track performance and maturity progression.
- **Maturity Alignment**: Used to identify critical focus areas necessary for transitioning to higher stages of service delivery maturity.

## Applications
- Benchmarking GBS organizations against industry best practices.
- Strategic planning for GBS transformation journeys.
- Improving the maturity of specific service delivery pillars within an enterprise.
- Supporting governance and enterprise-wide service optimization.
