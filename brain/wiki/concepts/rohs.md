# RoHS

## Definition
RoHS is a mandatory European Union regulatory framework that restricts the use of specific hazardous materials found in electrical and electronic equipment (EEE) to protect human health and the environment.

## Key Characteristics
- **Mandatory Compliance**: Acts as a regulatory instrument requiring adherence for products to enter the EU market.
- **Supply Chain Integration**: Forces the ICT sector and industrial value chains to integrate compliance checks into product design and life-cycle management.
- **Regulatory "Teeth"**: Demonstrates high enforcement strength, serving as a model for how global markets respond to rigorous compliance mandates.
- **Automated Workflow Driver**: Serves as a foundational driver for the development of automated compliance systems, often used alongside other regulatory frameworks.

## Applications
- **Product Design**: Integration of compliance checkpoints into early-stage engineering to ensure hazardous materials are excluded.
- **Life-cycle Management**: Tracking material composition throughout the entire supply chain to ensure ongoing regulatory adherence.
- **RegTech Architecture**: Serving as a benchmark for developing automated, data-driven compliance systems in complex global value chains.
