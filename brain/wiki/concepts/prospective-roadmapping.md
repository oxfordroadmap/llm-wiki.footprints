# Prospective roadmapping

## Definition
A methodology that systematically projects future regulatory and industrial milestones to engineer technology infrastructure ahead of market enforcement. It shifts the focus from retrospective tracking to active design, enabling organizations to build compliance capabilities proactively.

## Key Characteristics
- **Proactive Compliance Integration**: Integrates policy constraints directly into technical architectures during the design phase.
- **Negative Feedback Loops**: Transforms upstream physical vulnerabilities into mechanisms for environmental accountability.
- **Socio-technical Alignment**: Anchored in a socio-technical systems approach to bridge the gap between regulatory requirements and infrastructure capabilities.
- **Strategic Foresight**: Moves beyond retrospective reporting to model and prepare for future enforcement milestones.

## Applications
- Engineering infrastructure to meet upcoming environmental and industrial standards before they are legally enforced.
- Integrating policy-as-code into industrial frameworks to ensure continuous compliance.
- Designing circular, regenerative technical systems that account for long-term sustainability requirements.
