## Description
Professional Proxies function as autonomous agents representing specific corporate roles, such as Facility, Process Engineering, or Finance, resolving the conflict between data privacy and transparency demands in multi-stakeholder ecosystems. By operating within [[concepts/trusted-execution-environment-tee|Trusted Execution Environments]], they perform inline compliance checks and generate verifiable tokens without exposing sensitive raw manufacturing data. Building on this foundation, advanced Agentic AI workflows can now populate and validate Digital Product Passports in real time to ensure continuous adherence to regulations like [[concepts/safe-and-sustainable-by-design|Safe and Sustainable by Design]] and CBAM. These agents act as a bridge between the physical realities of production and the administrative requirements of international trade, allowing firms to navigate the [[concepts/data-sovereignty-paradox|Data Sovereignty Paradox]] effectively. By orchestrating complex data workflows while edge-intelligent hardware monitors real-world processes, these proxies convert regulatory pressures into competitive advantages.
