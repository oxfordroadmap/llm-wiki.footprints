# Technology Roadmapping

## Definition
Technology Roadmapping (TRM) is a flexible strategic planning technique that aligns technological developments with business goals and market needs. It serves as a visual or structured framework to map out the evolution of technologies, products, and services over a specific timeline, helping organizations navigate complex innovation landscapes.

## Key Characteristics
*   **Time-based visualization**: Tracks the progression of technologies against defined milestones and long-term objectives.
*   **Strategic Alignment**: Ensures that R&D investments are directly linked to market requirements and company strategy.
*   **Cross-functional integration**: Bridges the gap between technical departments, marketing, and executive leadership to foster a unified vision.
*   **Dynamic Nature**: Allows for iterative adjustments as new data emerges or market conditions change.

## Applications
*   **Strategic R&D Planning**: Used by organizations like [[entities/tsmc|TSMC]] to synchronize long-term semiconductor development with customer demand.
*   **Standardization Processes**: Employed by bodies like the [[entities/ieee-international-roadmap-for-devices-and-systems|IEEE IRDS]] to coordinate global industry efforts on device and system scaling.
*   **Innovation Management**: Facilitates the identification of technological "white spaces" and potential bottlenecks in product lifecycles.
