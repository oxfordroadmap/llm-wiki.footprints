## Description
GBS 5.0 represents the contemporary evolution of global business services, shifting from traditional, labor-arbitrage-based transactional models toward high-value, AI-driven, and human-centric service hubs. To support this transition, organizational maturity is often evaluated using the Business Services Maturity Model, which categorizes progress across 10 key operational dimensions. While GBS 5.0 emphasizes the integration of innovation ecosystems and sustainable intelligence, the maturity model provides a structured path for scaling foundational workflows. It is important to note that reaching the highest level of maturity is not a practical goal for every organization, as progress must be calibrated to specific strategic needs and systemic resilience requirements. By combining these approaches, enterprises can advance from basic cost-cutting to sophisticated, end-to-end outcome orientation.
