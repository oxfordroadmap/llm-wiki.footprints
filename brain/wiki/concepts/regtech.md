## Description
RegTech leverages advanced technologies, including Agentic AI and sovereign data spaces, to transform burdensome regulatory requirements into streamlined, data-driven workflows. By automating compliance tasks—such as those required by the [[concepts/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam|Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM)]] and [[concepts/safe-and-sustainable-by-design|Safe and Sustainable by Design (SSbD)]] frameworks—organizations can achieve real-time tracking, validation, and reporting of environmental and safety standards. This shift enables firms to align with global standards for data sovereignty, ensuring that organizations maintain control over how information is shared and utilized across Global Value Chains. Ultimately, RegTech serves as a critical mechanism for turning compliance obligations into growth points for sustainable, multi-stakeholder business models.
