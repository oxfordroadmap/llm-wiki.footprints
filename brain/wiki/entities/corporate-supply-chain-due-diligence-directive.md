## Additional Implementation Context
Recent assessments regarding the implementation of the directive indicate that for manufacturing and procurement organizations, the challenges remain structured across four critical stages: exploration, installation, initial implementation, and full implementation. These stages serve as the roadmap for firms seeking to align their operations with the requirements set forth by the directive.
