# Nvidia

## Basic Information
- Type: organization
- Source: [[sources/_id-401_current_version|_id-401_current_version]]

## Description
Nvidia is a leading semiconductor company and a primary driver of modern generative AI infrastructure. The company is central to the development of high-performance computing architectures that support large-scale AI deployments. Its operational strategy is defined by the [[concepts/six-layer-ssbd-reference-architecture|Six-Layer SSbD]] architecture—comprising Energy, Chips, Infrastructure, Models, and Applications—which functions as a linear model for AI scaling. The industry approach led by Nvidia is often critiqued for its extractive resource requirements and its limited focus on [[concepts/circular-economy|circular-economy]] principles within the AI value chain.
