## Description
Nvidia is a leading semiconductor company and a primary driver of modern generative AI infrastructure. The company is central to the development of high-performance computing architectures that support large-scale AI deployments, often framing AI as essential infrastructure comparable to electricity or the internet. Its operational strategy is defined by the [[concepts/six-layer-ssbd-reference-architecture|Six-Layer SSbD]] architecture, which functions as a linear model for AI scaling. To support its growth, the company actively lobbies for the expansion of electricity infrastructure within key semiconductor hubs like Taiwan. The industry approach led by Nvidia is often critiqued for its extractive resource requirements and its limited focus on [[concepts/circular-economy|circular-economy]] principles within the AI value chain, particularly concerning the semiconductor-petrochemical nexus.
