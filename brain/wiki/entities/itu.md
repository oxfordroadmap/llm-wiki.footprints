## Description
The International Telecommunication Union (ITU) is a specialized United Nations agency focused on information and communication technologies and serves as a global standard-setting body. Its ICT-Centric Innovation Ecosystem Toolkit provides a diagnostic framework for designing sustainable, multi-stakeholder digital projects, allowing researchers and Global Business Services (GBS) leaders to map project requirements against regional constraints. This toolkit is instrumental in aligning complex socio-technical interests and stakeholder engagement strategies within GBS initiatives. Furthermore, the ITU framework informs the design of green and digital business practices, helping to architect the future of the industry through systematic service design.
