## Description
MDEC is the government agency responsible for architecting the digital economy strategy for [[entities/malaysia|Malaysia]]. It is the primary architect of the 'Malaysia Digital' (MD) status and the GBS 5.0 framework, acting as a central coordinator for talent development, infrastructure, and policy alignment. Operating as an ecosystem orchestrator, the agency ensures that both foreign and domestic investments are aligned with national goals for digital and green transformation. It plays a pivotal role in positioning Malaysia as a regional gateway for high-value talent, IC design, and R&D activities, transitioning traditional shared service centers into sophisticated, AI-driven innovation hubs. Acting as a regime-influencing force, MDEC works alongside entrepreneurial support networks to compel Multinational Corporations (MNCs) to adapt their strategic roadmaps. It functions as a key entity under the Ministry of Digital (Malaysia) to lead and catalyze the nation's digital growth.
