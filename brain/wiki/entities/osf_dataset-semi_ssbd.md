# OSF_dataset-Semi_SSbD

## Basic Information
- Type: other
- Source: [[sources/_id-286_current_version|_id-286_current_version]]

## Description
The OSF_dataset-Semi_SSbD is a publicly accessible repository containing processed bibliometric data related to the intersection of AI, metrology, and [[concepts/safe-and-sustainable-by-design|Safe and Sustainable by Design]] (SSbD) in the semiconductor sector. It was compiled to ensure the reproducibility of the study's scoping review and to facilitate further academic research. The dataset includes thesaurus mappings and cleaned metadata derived from 1,465 documents sourced from [[entities/web-of-science|Web of Science]] and [[entities/scopus|Scopus]]. It serves as a foundational resource for researchers aiming to map conceptual structures within the semiconductor domain.
