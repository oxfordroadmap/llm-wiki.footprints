## Description
Bosch is a leading global supplier of technology and services, headquartered in Gerlingen, Germany. The company operates in several business sectors, including Mobility Solutions, Industrial Technology, Consumer Goods, and Energy and Building Technology. Bosch is known for its significant investments in research and development, particularly in the fields of semiconductor manufacturing and Internet of Things (IoT) solutions. The organization is a key stakeholder in the European semiconductor ecosystem, collaborating with partners to enhance regional production capabilities. *(No source content available for this page)*
