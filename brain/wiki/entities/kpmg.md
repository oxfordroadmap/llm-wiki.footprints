# KPMG

## Basic Information
- Type: organization
- Source: [[brain/raw/KPMG_2016-GBS.md]]

## Description
KPMG is a global network of professional services firms that provides audit, tax, and advisory services. The firm is recognized for its research into the evolution of [[concepts/global-business-services|Global Business Services]], most notably developing a five-stage maturity model based on extensive studies conducted since 2011. They utilize this framework to advise clients on restructuring business operations and optimizing shared services. Additionally, the firm frequently publishes thought leadership regarding business services transformation and outsourcing strategies.
