## Description
Scopus is a comprehensive abstract and citation database of peer-reviewed literature. It is frequently utilized alongside [[entities/web-of-science|Web of Science]] to capture a broader thematic spread, particularly reflecting applied industrial knowledge cycles through conference papers. The database serves as a foundational tool for bibliometric analysis and research mapping, providing deep insight into scientific and technical trends.
