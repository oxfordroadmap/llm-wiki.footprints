## Description
NXP Semiconductors is a global leader in the design and manufacturing of high-performance mixed-signal semiconductors. The company focuses on secure connectivity solutions for embedded applications, with a strong presence in the automotive, industrial, mobile, and communication infrastructure markets. NXP is a key partner in collaborative European semiconductor manufacturing initiatives, aiming to bolster the regional supply chain for advanced technologies. *(No source content available for this page)*
