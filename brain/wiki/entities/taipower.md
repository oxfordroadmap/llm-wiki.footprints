# Taipower

## Basic Information
- Type: organization
- Source: [[sources/_id-401_current_version|_id-401_current_version]]

## Description
Taipower is the state-owned utility company in Taiwan responsible for the generation and distribution of electricity. The organization is tasked with managing critical energy supply-demand imbalances, particularly in northern Taiwan, where it enforces regulatory ceilings on new data center grid applications. These operational policies act as a physical constraint on the expansion of resource-intensive [[entities/nvidia|NVIDIA]]-related AI infrastructure. The utility's role is central to the debate on the energy requirements of advanced computing, reflecting the broader challenges of the [[concepts/digitalization-paradox|Digitalization paradox]].
