# Malaysia

## Basic Information
- Type: location
- Source: [[sources/_id-306_current_version|_id-306_current_version]]

## Description
Malaysia has established itself as an 'Asia-Pacific digital gateway' through its [[concepts/gbs-5-0|GBS 5.0]] framework, focusing on high-value digital operations and R&D. The region, particularly Penang, is noted for its ability to integrate high-value functions such as IC design and R&D, positioning it as a competitor in the global technology value chain. The country leverages government-led initiatives to create resilient ecosystems that support both green and digital transformation, acting as a crucial node for multinational corporations seeking to navigate volatile global markets.
