## Description
Chang-Yi Kao is an academic researcher and faculty member at the Department of Computer Science and Information Management at [[entities/soochow-university|Soochow University]] in Taipei, Taiwan. He specializes in the integration of computer science frameworks within industrial management systems, contributing to contemporary research on [[concepts/safe-and-sustainable-by-design|Safe and Sustainable by Design]] and industrial data infrastructures. His scholarly work further explores the intersection of digital governance, regulatory technology, and supply chain transparency. He is a recognized member of the IEEE, focusing on how professional-led data orchestration can be applied to complex socio-technical systems.
