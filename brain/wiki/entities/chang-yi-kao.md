## Description
Chang-Yi Kao is an academic researcher affiliated with the Department of Computer Science and Information Management at [[entities/soochow-university|Soochow University]] in Taipei, Taiwan. He specializes in the integration of computer science frameworks within industrial management systems, contributing to contemporary research on [[concepts/safe-and-sustainable-by-design|Safe and Sustainable by Design]] and industrial data infrastructures. His work often explores the intersection of professional-led data orchestration and complex socio-technical systems.
