## Description
ASML is a major Dutch multinational corporation specializing in the production of photolithography systems for [[concepts/semiconductor-manufacturing|Semiconductor Manufacturing]]. It is identified as a critical player in the global semiconductor ecosystem, with extensive industrial R&D outputs captured in academic datasets. ASML is highlighted as a key stakeholder whose technical influence and collaborative efforts contribute to the development of interoperable semiconductor standards. Its presence underscores the industry-wide focus on bridging technological advancement with systemic sustainability goals.
