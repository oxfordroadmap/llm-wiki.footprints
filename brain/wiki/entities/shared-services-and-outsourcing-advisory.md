## Description
The Shared Services and Outsourcing Advisory (SSOA) is a specialized practice group within [[entities/kpmg|KPMG]] focused on enterprise business service transformation. The group collaborates with global organizations to implement [[concepts/gbs-5-0|Global Business Services]] (GBS) models designed to enhance value, operational agility, and sustainable performance. Complementing this, the KPMG Shared Services and Outsourcing Institute serves as the firm's central hub for research, thought leadership, and the repository of strategic frameworks and best practices. Together, these entities provide the essential methodological guidance for organizations navigating the complexities of GBS maturity and service delivery transformation.
