## Description
Penang is a prominent [[entities/malaysia|Malaysia]] region serving as a high-value technology and [[entities/global-business-services|GBS]] hub. Often referred to as the 'Silicon Valley of the East', it has attracted significant infrastructure investment to facilitate advanced functions like integrated circuit (IC) design and Research and Development (R&D). Its role is central to the digital economic strategy of [[entities/malaysia|Malaysia]] and its positioning within global value chains.
