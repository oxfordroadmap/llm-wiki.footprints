# PETRONAS

## Basic Information
- Type: organization
- Source: [[sources/_ids_agentic_current_version|_ids_agentic_current_version]]

## Description
PETRONAS is a major petrochemical firm based in Malaysia that represents the upstream segment of the semiconductor-petrochemical nexus. Through its acquisition of Perstorp, the firm is directly impacted by European Union sustainability regulations such as [[concepts/safe-and-sustainable-by-design|SSbD]] and [[concepts/sustainability-standards|CBAM]]. The organization is identified as a key actor for implementing sovereign data exchanges to ensure cross-border compliance within global value chains. It plays a critical role in the supply of materials required for wafer cleaning in semiconductor manufacturing.
