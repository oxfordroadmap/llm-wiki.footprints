# Stan Lepeak

## Basic Information
- Type: person
- Source: [[sources/kpmg_2016-gbs|KPMG_2016-GBS]]

## Description
Stan Lepeak is the Global Research Director of [[entities/kpmg|KPMG]] Management Consulting. He plays a key role in synthesizing research data, including the long-running [[concepts/gbs-5-0|Global Business Services]] Maturity Research Study, into actionable insights for clients. His work focuses on providing analytical rigor to the assessment of organizational maturity models and the evolution of global business delivery frameworks.
