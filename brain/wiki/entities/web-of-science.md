# Web of Science

## Basic Information
- Type: other
- Source: [[brain/raw/_id-286_current_version]]

## Description
Web of Science is a curated, high-impact bibliographic database widely utilized for academic and scientific research. In the context of the [[brain/raw/_id-286_current_version|SSbD Semiconductor Review]], it serves as a primary data source for mapping conceptual and social structures within semiconductor research. It provides a dataset with high citation impact, focused primarily on academic institutions and fundamental science. It is often compared with other bibliographic databases such as [[entities/scopus|Scopus]].
