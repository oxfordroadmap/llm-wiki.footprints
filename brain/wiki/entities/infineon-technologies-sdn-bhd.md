## Description
Infineon is a leading global semiconductor manufacturer recognized for its role in orchestrating complex semiconductor value chains. The company serves as a strategic stakeholder capable of leading regional [[concepts/international-data-spaces|International Data Spaces]] (IDS) hubs to establish uniform semantic ontologies across multi-tier supplier networks. It is deeply involved in cross-border compliance frameworks, specifically focusing on the integration of automated IDS connectors into existing industrial systems. Infineon acts as a key industry participant in driving accountability and real-time emissions tracking, aiming for verified embedded tracking across Tier-1 chemical and material suppliers within an 18-month roadmap.
