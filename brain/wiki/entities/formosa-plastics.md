# Formosa Plastics

## Basic Information
- Type: organization
- Source: [[sources/_ids_agentic_current_version|_ids_agentic_current_version]]

## Description
Formosa Plastics is a leading petrochemical producer based in Taiwan that supplies essential materials and chemicals to the global semiconductor manufacturing sector. As an upstream supplier, the company is subject to increasing regulatory and commercial pressures to disclose actual embedded carbon emissions under the [[concepts/sustainability-standards|Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM)]]. The firm's data practices represent a significant node in the implementation of sovereign data exchange architectures, which aim to reconcile upstream manufacturing complexity with downstream accountability. By participating in these frameworks, Formosa Plastics plays a critical role in the [[concepts/twin-transition|Twin Transition]] for industrial supply chains.
