## Description
Soochow University is a higher education institution based in Taipei, Taiwan. The university serves as a vital base for academic research, particularly within the Department of Computer Science and Information Management. It contributes significantly to studies concerning digital systems, information management, and academic-industry collaborations, including the development of RegTech frameworks for industrial sectors.
