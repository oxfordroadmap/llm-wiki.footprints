# LCY Chemical

## Basic Information
- Type: organization
- Source: [[brain/raw/_IDS_Agentic_Current_Version|_IDS_Agentic_Current_Version]]

## Description
LCY Chemical is a major chemical manufacturer based in Taiwan that specializes in supplying high-purity solvents, such as isopropyl alcohol (IPA), essential to the global [[concepts/environmental-sustainability-of-the-semiconductor-facilities|semiconductor industry]]. As an upstream participant in the semiconductor-petrochemical nexus, the company plays a critical role in the [[concepts/twin-transition|twin transition]] of supply chains. It faces direct requirements to verify actual carbon emissions data for compliance with [[concepts/sustainability-standards|international sustainability standards]] and trade regulations like the [[concepts/esg-compliance|Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM)]]. The firm's operational data serves as a practical case study for transitioning from default carbon values to verified, line-by-line reporting protocols within the [[concepts/international-data-spaces|International Data Spaces]] framework.
