# To Share or Not to Share: Orchestrating Trustworthy Data in Global Value Chains - Summary

## Source
- Original file: [[brain/raw/_IDS_Agentic_Current_Version.md]]
- Ingested: 2026-06-11

## Core Content
This article presents a framework for managing the intersection of regulatory compliance and industrial innovation within the semiconductor-petrochemical nexus. The authors, [[entities/han-teng-liao|Han-Teng Liao]] and [[entities/chang-yi-kao|Chang-Yi Kao]], propose an [[concepts/regtech|RegTech]] reference architecture grounded in the [[entities/international-data-spaces-association|International Data Spaces Association]] (IDSA) standards. By shifting from voluntary reporting frameworks like [[concepts/science-based-targets-initiative|SBTi]] to mandatory, product-level accountability required by the EU's [[concepts/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism|CBAM]] and [[concepts/safe-and-sustainable-by-design|SSbD]], the study provides a roadmap for digital circularity. The framework leverages [[concepts/digital-product-passport|Digital Product Passports]] and [[concepts/agentic-ai|Agentic AI]] to automate compliance data exchange across the [[entities/taipei-and-penang-technology-corridor|Taipei and Penang technology corridor]]. By integrating these technologies, industrial actors can transform regulatory burdens into competitive advantages, ensuring resilient and transparent value chains through sovereign data governance.

## Main Points
- The semiconductor-petrochemical value chain faces urgent regulatory pressure from the upcoming 2026 [[concepts/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism|CBAM]] mandate.
- There is a critical strategic difference between voluntary corporate pledges and mandatory, auditable product-level data accountability.
- [[concepts/digital-product-passport|Digital Product Passports]] are vital for ensuring traceability of emissions and safety profiles across global, multi-tier supplier networks.
- Implementing [[concepts/regtech|RegTech]] solutions through IDSA-compliant data spaces allows firms to turn mandatory compliance into a mechanism for long-term sustainability and financial leverage.
- [[concepts/agentic-ai|Agentic AI]] enables the automation of compliance data validation, significantly reducing the manual burden of environmental reporting.
- A [[concepts/prospective-roadmapping|prospective roadmapping]] approach allows companies to transition from reactive, linear technology stacks toward circular, governed industrial ecosystems.
