# From Stacks to Circuits: A Regenerative Socio-Technical Roadmap for AI Infrastructure within Planetary Boundaries - Summary

## Source
- Original file: brain/raw/_ID-401_Current_Version.md
- Ingested: 2026-06-10

## Core Content
This study, authored by [[entities/han-teng-liao|Han-Teng Liao]] and [[entities/karen-ang|Karen Ang]], proposes a [[concepts/regenerative-socio-technical-framework|Regenerative Socio-Technical framework]] to address the ecological and systemic challenges posed by the rapid scaling of generative AI. The authors critique the linear "electrons-to-tokens" model championed by [[entities/nvidia|Nvidia]] and [[entities/jensen-huang|Jensen Huang]], arguing that it exacerbates the [[concepts/jevons-paradox|Jevons Paradox]] and ignores physical limitations such as the [[concepts/memory-wall|Memory Wall]]. 

By integrating [[concepts/regenerative-accounting|Regenerative Accounting]] and the [[concepts/systemic-volatility-indicator|Systemic Volatility Indicator]], the roadmap reframes infrastructure as a metabolic system that internalizes ecological debt. The framework emphasizes compliance with emerging European standards like the [[concepts/sustainability-standards|Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism]] and [[concepts/safe-and-sustainable-by-design|Safe and Sustainable by Design]] guidelines, while leveraging [[concepts/digital-product-passports|Digital Product Passports]] to manage life-cycle impacts. The study also identifies regional grid constraints—highlighting the [[concepts/grid-paradox|Grid Paradox]] faced by utilities like [[entities/taipower|Taipower]]—and advocates for the adoption of [[concepts/energy-communities|Energy Communities]] to ensure sustainable industrial resilience within planetary boundaries.

## Main Points
- Generative AI infrastructure currently relies on linear, extractive "stack" architectures that prioritize token throughput over thermodynamic and ecological stability.
- The "electrons-to-tokens" model drives resource consumption exponentially, failing to account for the rebound effects characterized by the Jevons Paradox.
- The proposed RST framework mandates the internalization of ecological debt into corporate balance sheets through Regenerative Accounting.
- Real-time management of computational loads via Systemic Volatility Indicators can help synchronize data center operations with regional energy grid capacities.
- Strategic alignment with EU regulatory frameworks (CSDDD, SSbD) is necessary to reconcile high-performance computing growth with mandatory planetary boundaries.
