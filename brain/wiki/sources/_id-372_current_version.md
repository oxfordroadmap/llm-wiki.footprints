# Trustworthy Smart Fabs via Professional Proxies: Scaling Safe and Sustainable by Design (SSbD) through Industrial Data Spaces - Summary

## Source
- Original file: [[brain/raw/_ID-372_Current_Version.md]]
- Ingested: 2026-06-10

## Core Content
This research addresses the critical governance bottleneck in semiconductor manufacturing driven by the convergence of European regulations such as [[concepts/safe-and-sustainable-by-design|SSbD]], [[entities/corporate-supply-chain-due-diligence-directive|corporate-supply-chain-due-diligence-directive]], and [[concepts/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam|carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam]]. To overcome the limitations of manual, reactive auditing, the authors propose a zero-trust, [[concepts/industry-5-0|Industry 5.0]]-aligned architecture. The framework utilizes [[concepts/professional-proxies|Professional Proxies]]—autonomous, role-based agentic workflows operating within hardware-isolated [[concepts/trusted-execution-environment-tee|trusted-execution-environment-tee]]—to manage data hand-offs across a [[concepts/six-layer-ssbd-reference-architecture|Six-Layer SSbD Reference Architecture]]. 

By leveraging [[concepts/international-data-spaces|International Data Spaces]] (IDS) connectors and [[concepts/policy-as-code|Policy-as-Code]] engines like [[concepts/open-policy-agent|Open Policy Agent]], the system resolves the [[concepts/data-sovereignty-paradox|Data Sovereignty Paradox]]. This allows semiconductor fabs to export cryptographically verified compliance tokens without exposing sensitive process recipes or intellectual property. The methodology, structured as a [[concepts/relay-race-framework|Relay Race Framework]], transforms compliance from a retrospective burden into a proactive, evidence-based manufacturing capability.

## Main Points
- Advanced semiconductor manufacturing faces a governance crisis due to the intersection of complex regulatory demands and the need for data secrecy.
- The proposed architecture uses [[concepts/professional-proxies|Professional Proxies]] to enable real-time, autonomous regulatory reporting within [[concepts/trusted-execution-environment-tee|trusted-execution-environment-tee]].
- The [[concepts/data-sovereignty-paradox|Data Sovereignty Paradox]] is mitigated through [[concepts/international-data-spaces|International Data Spaces]], allowing verified compliance tokens to be shared without compromising proprietary [[concepts/virtual-metrology|Virtual Metrology]] or process data.
- The framework operationalizes sustainability via a [[concepts/relay-race-framework|Relay Race Framework]], ensuring seamless data integration from factory floor telemetry to corporate-level ESG reporting.
- The approach provides a scalable roadmap for resilient [[concepts/industry-5-0|Industry 5.0]] ecosystems that balance manufacturing productivity with net-zero compliance.
