# Orchestrating the Twin Transition in Multinational Corporations: Technology Roadmapping for Green and Digital Global Business Services - Summary

## Source
- Original file: brain/raw/_ID-306_Current_Version.md
- Ingested: 2026-06-10

## Core Content
This paper investigates the evolution of [[entities/global-business-services|Global Business Services]] (GBS) within multinational corporations, positioning them as "living laboratories" for the [[concepts/twin-transition|Twin Transition]]. By synthesizing [[concepts/technology-roadmapping|Technology Roadmapping]] with the [[concepts/ict-centric-innovation-ecosystem-toolkit|ICT-centric innovation ecosystem toolkit]], the authors identify how GBS units function as "[[concepts/operational-airlock|operational airlocks]]" that translate macro-level landscape pressures—such as carbon neutrality mandates—into niche [[concepts/machine-learning|Machine Learning]] and AI-native workflows. The study analyzes the strategic rise of "Middle Power" hubs in [[entities/poland|Poland]], [[entities/portugal|Portugal]], and [[entities/mdec|mdec]], highlighting how these regions utilize [[concepts/gbs-5-0|GBS 5.0]] frameworks to bypass the [[concepts/middle-income-trap|middle-income trap]]. The findings emphasize that successful digital and green integration requires a [[concepts/socio-technical-systems|Socio-Technical Systems]] approach, where human-centric organizational culture and technological agility are treated as co-dependent investments rather than sequential upgrades.

## Main Points
- GBS units act as "[[concepts/operational-airlock|operational airlocks]]" that mediate between global landscape pressures and internal niche innovations.
- The GBS domain is shifting from simple labor-arbitrage models toward high-value [[concepts/sustainable-intelligence|Sustainable Intelligence]] and ecosystem orchestration.
- [[entities/poland|Poland]], [[entities/portugal|Portugal]], and [[entities/mdec|mdec]] serve as crucial hubs that leverage high-value talent to mitigate [[concepts/middle-income-trap|middle-income traps]].
- A [[concepts/socio-technical-systems|Socio-Technical Systems]] perspective is essential to avoid the [[concepts/maturity-trap|maturity trap]] when implementing new technologies.
- Effective orchestration of the [[concepts/twin-transition|Twin Transition]] requires harmonizing human-centric [[concepts/industry-5-0|Industry 5.0]] strategies with AI-driven process normalization.
